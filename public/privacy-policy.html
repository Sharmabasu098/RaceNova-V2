RaceNova V2 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

RaceNova V2 is a mobile-first 3D arcade racing game developed for the Pi ecosystem. This Privacy Policy explains what information RaceNova V2 handles when you use the application.

1. Information we receive

When you choose to sign in with Pi, RaceNova V2 requests the Pi username authentication scope. The application may receive the Pi authentication result, including the username and a provider-specific user identifier supplied by the Pi SDK.

RaceNova V2 does not ask for your Pi wallet passphrase, secret phrase, private key, or other wallet credentials.

2. Game data

RaceNova V2 may store gameplay information needed to provide the game experience, such as selected car, owned cars, upgrades, campaign progress, and in-game coin balance. This data is used for gameplay, progression, and saving your game state.

3. Authentication and Pi services

Pi authentication is provided through the Pi SDK. Pi authentication data is used only for authentication and the associated RaceNova V2 user experience. Pi Network may process information according to its own policies and terms.

4. Data minimization

RaceNova V2 is designed to request only information necessary for its functionality. We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information such as phone numbers, postal addresses, contacts, or unrelated profile information.

5. Payments and blockchain data

If Pi payment features are introduced or enabled in a future version, applicable transaction information may be processed through Pi Network services as required to complete and verify the transaction. RaceNova V2 will not request or store a user's wallet passphrase or secret phrase.

6. Third-party services

RaceNova V2 may use Pi Network SDK and related Pi services. Those services are operated by Pi Network and may have their own privacy terms and policies.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit access to authentication and gameplay information. Authentication credentials and wallet secrets are not requested or intentionally stored by RaceNova V2.

8. Children's privacy

RaceNova V2 is not specifically directed to children. Users should use the application in accordance with applicable age requirements and the terms of the Pi ecosystem.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RaceNova V2 functionality, authentication, payments, or applicable requirements change.

10. Contact

For questions about RaceNova V2 privacy, please use the official RaceNova V2 developer/support contact made available through the Pi Developer Portal or the project's official repository.
